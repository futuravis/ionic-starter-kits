Design note

CODE EXISTSDetection and rotation shipped in v0.03 and are tested. Only the screen and the manual override are new.

Why a human still has to look: the rotation is inferred by running the face detector at all four quarter turns and taking the most confident. There is no usable EXIF — all 40 files report orientation: 1, including the sideways ones. A frame with no face is deliberately left alone, so landscapes, detail shots and rings will never be rotated automatically even when they need it.

Only frames that changed are listed. Showing all 40 would bury the 15 that need checking.

Analyse

Measuring sharpness, exposure, contrast, colour and noise, then comparing each frame against the rest of the batch.

Presence, size, lighting and focus on people. Adds about one second per 40 photos.

Closed eyes and expressions are not detected — the model returns five landmarks with no eyelid or mouth-shape data.

Format

Convert to a vertical format for reels and stories. Optional — skip it to keep every frame as shot.

How to handle wide frames

25 of your 40 frames are landscape. A 3:2 frame cannot become 9:16 without either padding or cropping — there is no third option.

Fill — keep everything

the whole photo
100% kept

The frame sits whole on a 9:16 canvas. The surround is a blurred wash of the photo's own colours.

centre strip only
38% kept

Edge to edge with no padding, centred on the faces. 62% of the width is discarded.

100% kept

No conversion. Portrait frames are already vertical and may not need this at all.

1920 keeps detail crisp on a phone without huge files.

Design note

CODE EXISTSThe converter shipped in v0.03 with both modes, and is tested. Nothing is wired to the UI yet.

Why this step must come after Analyse, not before. The Fill mode adds a large blurred wash around the frame. If that ran before measurement, the wash would be measured as part of the photograph — dragging down sharpness and contrast and corrupting every score in the batch. Format has to be an export operation.

To be clear about what Fill is not: it does not invent more floor, wall or sky. That is generative outpainting and needs a generative model; this runs on OpenCV offline. It is a backdrop derived from the photo's own colours.
